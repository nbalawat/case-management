## What it is
Money that arrives in an account and leaves again within hours or days, with little left behind. The account acts as
a pass-through: often a money mule recruited online, a shell company, or a compromised account.

## Indicators
- Incoming transfers followed by outgoing wires or P2P payments of nearly the same total within 72 hours.
- Many unrelated senders, or payments onward to high-risk jurisdictions.
- A newly opened account, or a long-dormant one, that suddenly turns over large sums.
- Balances that fall back to near zero after each cycle.

## Mitigating factors
- Payroll, escrow, property completion or treasury sweeps with documentation.
- A business whose model is collection and onward payment (a payment agent with the right licences).

## What to do
Trace the funds one hop in each direction. Check counterparties against other alerts and cases. Look for links
to known mules. Consider a hold where funds are still present and the victim can be identified.
