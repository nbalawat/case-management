## Review cycle
High-risk customers are reviewed every year, medium every three years and low every five. A trigger can bring a
review forward: adverse media, a change of ownership or control, a new high-risk product, or a risk-rating change.

## Ratings and overrides
The risk model proposes a band. An analyst may override it with a written reason. An override that lowers the band
needs a second person's approval. Overrides are sampled by QA each month.

## Enhanced due diligence
Politically exposed persons, customers in high-risk jurisdictions and high-risk businesses need enhanced due
diligence: source of wealth and source of funds, senior approval at onboarding, and closer transaction monitoring.
