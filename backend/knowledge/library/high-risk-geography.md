## What it is
Wires and transfers involving countries on the FATF lists of jurisdictions under increased monitoring or subject to a
call for action, or countries the client's own country-risk model rates high.

## Indicators
- Payments with no clear business purpose to or from high-risk jurisdictions.
- Round-sum wires, or a series of wires just below internal review thresholds.
- Use of intermediaries in third countries with no link to the customer's business.

## What to do
Confirm the customer's stated business explains the corridor. Request invoices or contracts for trade-related
payments. Escalate to enhanced due diligence where the activity is new or growing.
