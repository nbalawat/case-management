## What it is
A fraudster gains control of a genuine customer's account or card, usually through phishing, SIM swap or credential
stuffing, and moves money out.

## Indicators
- A change of phone number, email or device shortly before unusual payments.
- Logins from a new location or at unusual hours, followed by new payees.
- Payments well above the customer's usual amounts, often in quick succession.

## What to do
Hold online access and pending outgoing payments. Contact the customer through a channel on file before the change.
Start a recall with the receiving bank as soon as possible. Treat the customer as a victim: open a dispute and check
the credit and recovery deadlines that apply.
