## The decision
File a suspicious activity report when, after review, the activity has no apparent lawful purpose or is not the sort
in which the customer would normally engage, and the bank knows of no reasonable explanation. The investigator
recommends; the MLRO or a delegate decides.

## Deadlines
In the United States a SAR is due within 30 calendar days of initial detection of facts that may form a basis for
filing. If no suspect is identified, the deadline can be extended by up to 30 more days, to 60 days in all.
Continuing activity is reviewed and reported every 90 days, with the report filed within 120 days of the last one.

## The narrative
Say who, what, when, where and why it is suspicious, in that order. Name the accounts, amounts and dates. Explain
what is unusual about them for this customer. Do not speculate beyond the facts. Keep the supporting documents with
the case for five years.

## Confidentiality (no tipping off)
Never tell the customer, or anyone outside those who need to know, that a SAR is being considered or has been filed.
