## Placing a hold
A hold on a payment, account, card or online access is placed only while a case is worked. Each hold has a reason
and a review date: two business days for fraud holds, one business day for sanctions holds pending a decision.

## Review
A hold past its review date is escalated to the team lead. Holds are not left in place to save work: either the case
moves forward, or the hold is released with a reason.

## Release
Releasing a held payment needs a second person (four eyes). Sanctions holds on a true match are never released
without a licence.
