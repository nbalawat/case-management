## What it is
A customer, counterparty or payment party whose name is similar to an entry on a sanctions list the client screens
against (for example OFAC's SDN list, the UN consolidated list, the EU and UK lists).

## How to decide
- Compare strong identifiers first: date of birth, nationality, address, registration number. One clear mismatch on
  a strong identifier is usually enough to discount the match.
- Weak similarity on a common name, with no other matching detail, is a false positive.
- Record the reason for every decision; a second person approves any release of a held payment.

## When it is a true match
Keep the payment or account blocked. Under OFAC rules, report blocked property within 10 business days
(31 CFR 501.603) and rejected transactions within 10 business days (31 CFR 501.604). Do not release funds without a
licence.
